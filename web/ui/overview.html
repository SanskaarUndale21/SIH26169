What happens every frame, and what you can replace

1

Observe

The virtual 640 × 480 camera renders its 4° × 3° view of the scene at 30 Hz, with the selected noise, weather, jitter and drift applied.

2

Acquire

A detector finds beacon-like spots: our learned CNN or classical difference-of-Gaussians, or yours. With an on-board-computer cue, the camera first slews to the predicted direction and scans around it. Swappable.

3

Estimate

An interacting multiple-model filter blends straight, turning and erratic motion models to predict where the beacon is, even between missed detections.

4

Point

A PID loop slews pan and tilt within the 5°/s limits to centre the beacon. If it's lost, a spiral then raster search sweeps until it's found again.
